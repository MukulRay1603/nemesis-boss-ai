HOW THE AI WORKS

01 OBSERVE

14 numbers

Health of both fighters, positions, distance, phase, stamina, the opponent's velocity, its own last move and the fight clock.

02 DECIDE

PPO policy

A two-layer network trained with PPO, exported to ONNX and run in Unity Sentis. It outputs a probability for each move and samples one.

03 ACT

State machine

The chosen move plays out through fixed, readable states: telegraph, attack, recovery or dodge. The policy picks what; the state machine controls how.

04 ESCALATE

Three phases

As its health drops the boss speeds up and its recovery window shrinks, and its arena lights change colour with it.

Reading the Boss AI card

Next move shows the move the policy just picked and how likely it was. Low numbers happen: the policy samples, so a 9% dodge is sometimes the pick.

Last 30 seconds is a tick for every decision, coloured by move (attack dodge special idle), with markers where the boss changes phase.

Mix shows how often it chose each move in each phase, so you can see its behaviour shift as it gets hurt.
